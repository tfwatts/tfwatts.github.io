## Overview

This reference map depicts Sonoma County, California, and four of its primary geographic layers: rivers, state and US highways, rail lines, and state parks. These features are set against a shaded-relief hillshade derived from a 10-meter digital elevation model, which emphasizes the county's terrain and makes it stand out from the surrounding region. An inset locator map situates Sonoma County within the broader context of the state of California.
