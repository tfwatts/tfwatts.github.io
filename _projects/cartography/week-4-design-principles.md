## Overview

This is a general reference map of California that locates the state’s key physical and cultural features, including major highways, cities, rivers and lakes, urban areas, and counties, against its neighboring states and Mexico. The assignment’s intent was to apply multilayer cartographic design principles, with particular emphasis on typography and labeling: establishing a clear visual hierarchy, differentiating highway types (Interstate, U.S., and state routes) through distinct shields, and placing labels according to best cartographic practice while removing duplicates. Distinct type treatments give each kind of feature its own voice, using upright labels for places, italics for water, and spaced capitals for areas, so the map reads clearly despite a dense label load. Data was drawn from Natural Earth, U.S. Census TIGER/Line, and USGS NHD, and the map was produced in ArcGIS Pro using the California (Teale) Albers projection
