## Overview

This map presents the results of the 2012 U.S. presidential election across Georgia’s 159 counties using a bivariate graduated symbol design. Each county is represented by a point symbol whose color indicates the winning party, blue for Obama and red for Romney, and whose size reflects the county’s total population. Callout annotations highlight the counties where each candidate earned his highest vote share, Clayton County for Obama at 85 percent and Banks County for Romney at 87 percent. The assignment called for a bivariate graduated symbol map using point symbology rather than a choropleth, so that size and color could carry two variables at once without distorting the data by county land area. Projected in UTM Zone 17N, the map pairs a graduated size legend with a party color key to keep both variables clear to the reader.
